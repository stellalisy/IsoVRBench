IsoVRBench example data
==========================================================================

Source   Prompts are from VidProM (VidProM_semantic_unique.csv),
         filtered and deduplicated into our prompt pool v2.0.
Contents videos/<pid>/ holds one clip per intervention. s50.mp4 is the
         anchor; each other file is one controlled change to it, and the
         pair (anchor, variant) is one benchmark item.

Every prompt here carries all eight interventions. Two things disqualify
a prompt from appearing at all: wording that offers fewer than three
disjoint attribute swaps, which leaves no edited prompt for ta_attrib,
and an anchor that moves too little for a reordering to be visible,
which leaves no shuffle pair.

  s50.mp4             anchor, 50 denoising steps
  vq_steps_k8.mp4     vq_steps, 8 denoising steps, prompt and seed fixed
  mq_gen.mp4          mq_gen, motion between real keyframes regenerated
  mq_hold.mp4         mq_hold, frames of the anchor held, giving stuttering motion
  shuffle.mp4         shuffle, anchor cut into six segments and reordered
  size_i2v.mp4        size_i2v, Wan2.2-TI2V-5B continuing the anchor's first frame
  size_5b.mp4         size_5b, Wan2.2-TI2V-5B from the prompt
  size_1p3b.mp4       size_1p3b, Wan2.1-T2V-1.3B
  ta_attrib.mp4       ta_attrib, three prompt attributes rewritten, then rendered
